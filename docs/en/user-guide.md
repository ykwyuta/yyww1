# User Guide

The same content as the in-game Help (F1).

## Goal

The Great War begins with the Sarajevo assassination on June 28, 1914. Defeat every state outside your alliance that is at war with you to win. If no victor emerges by June 28, 1919 (the day of the Treaty of Versailles), the angels descend, take every city within half a year, and the game ends. Time flows the same for every state (1 month = 100 s).

## Outbreak

The July Crisis declarations (Austria on Serbia, Germany on Russia, France and Belgium, Britain on Germany...) happen automatically on their historical dates. The Ottoman Empire, Italy, Bulgaria, Portugal, Romania and Greece also enter on their historical dates. There is no peace between the alliances. Declare war on a neutral and it joins the enemy side.

## Resources

Food (farm), wood (cut forests), iron (mine). Workers carry them to your cities, which also pay tax. Production and resources are richest at the outbreak and decline the longer the war lasts.

## Population and conscription

Workers plus soldiers are limited (capturing enemy cities raises the limit; losing cities lowers it). Soldiers are drafted from workers: every soldier trained at a barracks takes one worker. The bigger the army, the thinner the economy. Soldiers can be demobilized back into workers (power drops to 2/3). Soldiers eat food just by existing (upkeep).

## Cities

Only the historical cities exist; no new ones can be founded. A state that loses all its cities falls.

## Public opinion

Opinion falls while you keep losing more than the enemy, keep too many soldiers for your population (heavy military spending), run out of food, or lose cities. In the empires (Germany, Austria-Hungary, the Ottomans, Russia, Bulgaria), opinion falling near 0 brings revolution and surrender (its cities go to the enemy). Democracies have no revolutions, but below 50 strikes cut production.

## Units

Every state can build infantry (rifles), artillery (long range, strong against infantry and buildings) and cavalry (fast, strong against artillery). Some have special units: tanks (Britain from Sept 1916, France from Apr 1917, the US from 1918), stormtroopers (Germany from July 1916), mountain troops (Italy, Austria-Hungary, France, Switzerland), U-boats (Germany, Austria-Hungary) and destroyers (Britain, France, Italy, Russia, the US). Tanks, U-boats and destroyers are weapons: select soldiers and press ‘Crew …’ (B / U) to make one (a tank takes 5 soldiers, a U-boat 4, a destroyer 6; tanks inside your territory, warships next to one of your naval bases). The soldiers become the weapon and can never be released.

## Crossing the sea

Order troops across the sea and they board transports at the nearest friendly port (or beach) and land at the port or beach nearest the goal. U-boats can sink transports. To invade Britain from Germany, for example, select soldiers and right-click a British city (London, say): they march to a home port (blue mark), sail, land on the British coast (orange mark) and attack the city.

## Colonies and convoys

Britain and France receive resources and workers by ship from their colonies (Canada, India, Africa, Algeria...) and from America. U-boats can sink them; destroyers hunt U-boats.

## America

The United States cannot be chosen. If it is obstructed — its neutral ships sunk, for example — its war fever rises, and at 100 it joins the Entente. Germany can begin unrestricted submarine warfare on the Diplomacy screen (sinking American ships too, at the risk of bringing America in).

## Trench lines

With only workers selected, press Trench (R) to dig one. It fires on approaching enemies and cuts damage to friendly troops beside it by 30%. Up to 4 plus 2 per city (at most 60).

## Historical events

The Miracle of the Marne, poison gas, the Lusitania, Verdun, the Somme, the Brusilov Offensive, the Zimmermann Telegram, the Spring Offensive, the Spanish flu and more happen on their dates. See them in the Chronicle (F3).

## National traits

Each state has historical traits (see the title screen and the Chronicle).

## Time stops while commanding

Selecting your own units stops time; it resumes when you give an order or clear the selection.

## Fog of war

You see only around your own and your allies' workers, soldiers and buildings. Every city (where it is and who holds it) is known from the start.

## Controls

- Left click: select   Drag: box select   Double click: select all of a type (works on barracks and cities too; training queues in each)   Shift: add to selection
- Right click: move / attack / gather (forest, mine, farm) / help build   Right click with a building selected: rally point
- A: attack-move   S: stop   T: train / educate   C: convert soldier ⇔ worker   Q/W/E/R: build / train
- Hold Ctrl while placing buildings: reserve builds (keep placing)
- Ctrl+number: save group   Number: recall group   .: idle worker   Home: go to capital
- Wheel: zoom   Middle drag / arrow keys / screen edge: scroll   Minimap click: jump
- Space: pause   + / -: speed   Esc: cancel   F2: Diplomacy   F3: Chronicle   F4: War   F5: save / load   F11: fullscreen   Ctrl++ / Ctrl+−: text size
